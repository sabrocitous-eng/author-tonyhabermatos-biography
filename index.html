Tony Haber-Matos

Author · U.S. Navy veteran · Founder & Principal, Casa Santiago LLC and Qvivo Brands Inc.

Biography

Tony Haber-Matos is a Cuban-American author and entrepreneur born in Santiago de Cuba (1973), founder of Casa Santiago LLC and Qvivo Brands Inc. in Miami. His novel LOST INNOCENCES: The Pact (Oreate–Casa Santiago Press, October 21, 2026), 400 pages and 39 chapters, is set in contemporary Havana, where three schoolboys at Colegio San Jerónimo make a bargain that follows them into adulthood. Who gets to decide? Published in seven languages and available on Amazon, Barnes & Noble, and Google Play Books. Find him on LinkedIn at https://www.linkedin.com/in/tony-haber/ and on Instagram at @tonyhaberofficial.
